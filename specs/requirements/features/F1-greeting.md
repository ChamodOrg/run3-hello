# Greeting

## Purpose

A page that shows the fixed message "Hello, World!" to any visitor.

## User Stories

- F1.1 As a visitor, I open the app and see the message "Hello, World!".
- F1.2 As a visitor, I see the greeting immediately on the first page, without signing in or clicking anything.

## Decisions

- The message text is fixed: "Hello, World!".
- The app is a single page; there is no navigation.

## Out of Scope

- Personalised greetings or entering a name.
- Choosing a language or changing the message.
